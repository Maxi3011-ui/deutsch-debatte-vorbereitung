## Design Context

### Users
Maxi prepares for a German classroom debate. The finished source PDF is already the preparation material. The primary device is an Apple Watch; phone and desktop should also work.

### Brand Personality
Clear, calm, practical. Continue the existing personal preparation sites, with no promotional content.

### Aesthetic Direction
Warm paper and restrained green on large screens, dark OLED-friendly watch layout. Prioritize native readable type and generous tap targets over decorative design.

### Design Principles
- Choose one of two topics and one of four positions at the top.
- Render only the complete chosen role package, followed by all general guidance.
- Preserve the supplied wording, examples, role distinctions and facts.
- Support very narrow displays without horizontal scrolling or permanent overlays.
- Keep the document immediately readable without expanding many panels.
